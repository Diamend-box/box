# DarkSea — Canon

The story the sea tells, as decided by Wyatt (Jul 18, 2026). Everything
below is the reference for mob names, island decoration, loot flavor and
future bosses.

## The Naxome

A civilization that once lived across these islands — the **Naxians**. The
ruins the generator scatters are theirs: the dwellings and graves by the
monolith, the watchtower garrisons, the grotto shrines, the harbor towns now
under the Raging Deep. They were not warlike; the sea was kind then.

## The Mariphage

An ancient curse, long buried, that behaves like a plague — a virus in the
water itself. It takes the mind first, then the flesh, then everything.
The zone effects players feel (poison, weakness, wither, darkness) are the
Mariphage's ambient touch; sea armor is what keeps it out. Infection deepens
with distance from the calm center, because that is where its keepers are
strongest.

## The Order of the Soul

The cult of the abyssal monolith. They are reseeding the Mariphage
deliberately, ring by ring, to revive a primordial power sleeping beneath
the Abyssal Reaches. The cult remains found on every island — candle rings,
skull altars, offering niches, glyph steles — are their work-sites. Ranks
climb with the rings, styled **"Vironic"** (name pending Wyatt's final
call): Initiate, Acolyte, Templar, Lord. The Order walks unharmed among the
Cursed; the plague knows its keepers.

## The enemies

| Ring | The Cursed (Mariphage victims) | The Order of the Soul |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · The Wild Sea | **Crazed Sailor** — mind gone, axe remembered | **Vironic Initiate** — outer-watch recruit, crossbow |
| 2 · The Raging Deep | **Mutated Naxian** — townsperson mid-change, harpoon | **Vironic Acolyte** — proven zealot, soul-sapping axe |
| 3 · The Midnight Waters | **Transmuted Naxian** — burned to blackened bone | **Vironic Templar** — war-priest, bottled plague |
| 4 · The Abyssal Reaches | **Naxian Abomination** — no longer Naxian at all | **Vironic Lord** — high priest of the monolith |

The Cursed grow more common the shallower you sail; the Order's share grows
with depth. Lower-ring enemies stray into deeper rings as rare leftovers
(the spawner's minimum-tier rule), so the deep sea still shows you what the
plague's early stages looked like.

## The boss (unnamed)

Only the Abyssal Reaches has a boss: the **half-revived primordial** the
Order serves — awake enough to kill, not yet awake enough to win. Name,
design and mechanics are their own round; one slot is reserved in the
Mythic pack and in `mobs.yml` tier 4.
